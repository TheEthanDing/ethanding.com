Sources & method
The extended chronology is cross-checked against the Metropolitan Museum’s Ancient Greece, 1000 BCE–1 CE and Ancient Greece, 1–500 CE timelines, with the Diadochi chapter following Livius’ chronology. City coordinates and identifications are cross-checked against the Pleiades ancient-world gazetteer. Character medallions without a securely sourced likeness use clearly editorial monograms rather than invented portraits. Coastlines, lakes, and river centerlines use 1:50m Natural Earth vector data; mountain chains are generalized editorial ridgelines. Ancient control boundaries, routes, positions, battles, and army encampments are approximate reconstructions. Several dates, army strengths, and movements remain disputed.
