# Atreides Data

## Brand idea

**Sovereign operational intelligence.**

Atreides Data turns fragmented, regulated, legacy, and on-premises data into a shared operational model that people and software can act on. Customers retain control of their data, workflow logic, and institutional intellectual property.

## Positioning

For mission-critical organizations whose most valuable data is fragmented across difficult systems, Atreides Data is the sovereign operational platform that unifies data into a governed ontology and turns it into executable workflows—without surrendering ownership of the logic that makes the organization unique.

## Core message

**Make complexity executable.**

Supporting line: **Your data. Your logic. Your advantage.**

Supporting claims:

- Integrate without rip-and-replace.
- Model the real organization, not an idealized schema.
- Turn governed context into action.
- Deploy across cloud, edge, and on-premises environments.
- Keep control of data, workflows, models, and derived IP.

## Logo concept

The **Sovereign Node** combines an architectural `A`, a protected central node, and two converging paths. It represents disconnected systems resolving into one governed operational model. The mark should remain flat, geometric, and high-contrast; avoid glossy effects, ornamental sci-fi styling, shields, locks, eyes, globes, or circuit-board motifs.

## Color

| Role | Name | Hex | Use |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Primary | Obsidian | `#0B0D0C` | Core backgrounds, product canvas |
| Primary light | Bone | `#E8E1D4` | Type, paper, light surfaces |
| Signal | Oxide | `#E65D2F` | Active states, calls to action, protected node |
| Operational | Mineral | `#65B8A6` | Healthy states, verified relationships |
| Neutral | Slate | `#627079` | Secondary UI, diagrams, metadata |

Oxide is a signal, not a wash. Mineral communicates state and trust. Obsidian and Bone carry most of the identity.

## Typography

- **Geist Sans** — headlines, navigation, product UI, and primary copy.
- **Source Serif 4** — editorial moments, point-of-view writing, and restrained contrast.
- **IBM Plex Mono** — data labels, audit states, coordinates, identifiers, and system metadata.

Use large, plainspoken headlines; compact sans-serif body copy; and sparse monospace annotations. Avoid futuristic display faces.

## Graphic system

- Thin ontology connections show relationships between operational objects.
- Protected boundary frames indicate sovereignty and governed scope.
- Contour lines suggest difficult real-world terrain and complex operating environments.
- Square nodes represent source systems; circular nodes represent governed entities; arrows represent executable action.
- Small status labels use direct language such as `VERIFIED`, `ACTIVE`, `ENFORCED`, and `EXECUTED`.

## Voice

Atreides sounds direct, controlled, and technically credible. It speaks about outcomes and operating realities, not abstract transformation.

Prefer:

- “Unify the systems you already run.”
- “Model how your organization actually works.”
- “Put governed intelligence into operation.”
- “Keep the logic that makes your organization yours.”

Avoid inflated AI language, mystical metaphors, fear-based security copy, and generic phrases such as “unlock the power of your data.”

## Brand personality

- Sovereign, not closed
- Operational, not analytical
- Severe, not cold
- Precise, not academic
- Field-ready, not theatrical
- Confident, not grandiose

## Recommended homepage hierarchy

1. **Make complexity executable.**
2. Unify fragmented data into a governed operational model—then build workflows that act on it, in your environment and on your terms.
3. Primary action: **See the platform**
4. Secondary action: **Discuss a deployment**
5. Proof sequence: integrate → model → govern → execute
