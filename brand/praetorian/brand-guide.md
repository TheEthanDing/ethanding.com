# Praetorian

## Brand idea

**Sovereign execution.**

Praetorian is the operating system for mission-critical organizations whose most important data is fragmented, regulated, legacy, or on premises. It turns that data into a shared ontology and then into governed workflows and action—while the customer keeps control of the data, logic, workflows, models, and resulting intellectual property.

## Positioning

For institutions with complex operations and hard-to-reach data, Praetorian is the sovereign operational platform that creates one permission-aware model of the organization and makes it executable. Unlike closed platforms that accumulate dependency, Praetorian leaves the customer with durable institutional capability.

## Core message

**Your data. Your logic. Your command.**

Supporting line: **From record to action.**

Supporting claims:

- Integrate without rip-and-replace.
- Model the organization as it actually operates.
- Trace every answer and action to its source.
- Keep humans in command of consequential decisions.
- Deploy where the customer operates: cloud, edge, isolated network, or on premises.
- Keep ownership of data, ontology, workflow logic, models, and derived IP.

## Logo concept

The **Fortified Threshold** combines three ideas in one geometric mark: a protected boundary, an open portal, and a branching data path. Its negative space suggests a capital `P` without relying on a literal Roman symbol. The portal remains visibly open: protection is not lock-in.

Keep the mark flat, architectural, and high contrast. Avoid helmets, soldiers, eagles, laurels, swords, columns, padlocks, or generic shield badges.

## Color

| Role | Name | Hex | Use |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Foundation | Carbon | `#11110F` | Product canvas, core backgrounds, dark wordmark |
| Institutional | Porphyry | `#4B2331` | Brand fields, photography treatment, depth |
| Command | Imperial Lac | `#8F2D35` | Active states, emphasis, decisive action |
| Ground | Travertine | `#E7DDC9` | Light surfaces, editorial pages, primary reverse type |
| Heritage | Bronze | `#9C7449` | Rules, seals, premium detail, provenance |
| Verified | Verdigris | `#5D8A7D` | Healthy states, verified relationships, system trust |

Carbon and Travertine carry most layouts. Porphyry gives the company its own institutional color world. Imperial Lac is controlled emphasis rather than a page wash. Bronze and Verdigris are functional accents derived from the aging of protective metal.

## Typography

- **Monument: Canela / Source Serif 4** — large editorial headlines, point of view, institutional authority.
- **System: Geist / Inter** — product UI, navigation, body copy, operational clarity.
- **Signal: IBM Plex Mono** — provenance, permissions, identifiers, source systems, timestamps, audit states.

Use high-contrast scale: monumental serif statements against compact sans-serif explanation and sparse monospace evidence. Avoid futuristic display faces and faux-Roman inscription fonts.

## Graphic system

- Hairline boundary frames define governed scope and custody.
- Open corners and thresholds communicate controlled access without enclosure.
- Ontology branches use few nodes and meaningful hierarchy, not decorative network noise.
- Source labels, timestamps, approvals, and permission states make the interface feel evidentiary.
- Bas-relief paper, travertine grain, oxidized bronze, and industrial control hardware add tactile history without historical reenactment.
- Documentary imagery should show the difficult systems and real environments where work happens: control rooms, equipment cabinets, field operations, records, and human judgment.

## Voice

Praetorian sounds controlled, direct, and technically literate. It speaks about custody, execution, and operating reality rather than abstract transformation.

Prefer:

- “Unify the systems you already run.”
- “Make your operating model executable.”
- “Every action traceable to its source.”
- “Deploy where you operate.”
- “Keep the logic that makes the institution yours.”

Avoid mystical AI language, conquest metaphors, fear-based security copy, Roman catchphrases, and generic claims about “unlocking data.”

## Brand personality

- Sovereign, not isolationist
- Formidable, not aggressive
- Institutional, not bureaucratic
- Operational, not merely analytical
- Severe, not cold
- Classical in proportion, not historical in costume
- Confident, not grandiose

## Recommended homepage hierarchy

1. **Your data. Your logic. Your command.**
2. Turn fragmented, regulated data into a shared operational model—then build governed workflows that act on it, in your environment and on your terms.
3. Primary action: **See the system**
4. Secondary action: **Discuss a deployment**
5. Proof sequence: connect → model → govern → execute → retain
6. Product evidence: provenance, permissions, source systems, human approvals, action state

## Reference-language synthesis

- Use cinematic, real-world operational imagery with interface overlays that add context rather than decoration.
- Use editorial scale, restrained grids, thin rules, and live operational instrumentation.
- Keep the palette, mark, Roman material cues, ownership message, and product metaphors uniquely Praetorian.
