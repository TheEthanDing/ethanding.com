# Praetorian — Identity V2

## Theme

**The Silent Instrument**

Praetorian is powerful infrastructure that operates inside the customer's environment, acts with controlled force, and leaves the customer sovereign. The identity should feel like an institutional sigil that existed before the software: inherited, disciplined, compact, and credible when stamped into machinery.

## Why the first mark failed

The original shield-and-network construction explained too much and signified too little. Shield outlines, circuit branches, and protected nodes are category clichés. They made Praetorian look like a conventional cybersecurity product rather than a company with a distinct institutional worldview.

## Emblem

The **Silent Scorpion** is an abstract, asymmetric scorpion rune derived from a historical Praetorian insignia. It uses a hooked segmented tail, a compact body, one decisive negative-space cut, and a small number of blunt geometric masses.

The emblem is not an illustration. It should read in this order:

1. Unfamiliar institutional sigil
2. Watchful or guarded presence
3. Scorpion
4. A possible abstract `P`, discovered rather than announced

The mark must always work in one color. Do not add internal data nodes, network lines, shields, gradients, dimensional effects, or ornamental borders.

## Design-language principles

- Use one hard silhouette rather than a collection of product metaphors.
- Let asymmetry create character and recognition.
- Make every application feel physical enough to be stamped, etched, printed, or stenciled.
- Keep the interface evidentiary: source, permission, approval, and execution state.
- Pair ancient-future material cues with contemporary institutional typography.
- Treat mystery as restraint, not vagueness.

## Color

| Role | Name | Hex | Use |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Foundation | Night | `#121215` | Product canvas, dark fields, primary mark |
| Institutional | Imperial Plum | `#352331` | Brand surfaces, photography treatment, depth |
| Secondary | Praetorian Violet | `#68445D` | Selected states, diagrams, editorial emphasis |
| Ground | Bone | `#E8E0D1` | Light surfaces, reverse type, archival applications |
| Material | Old Bronze | `#9A784B` | Rules, hardware details, provenance, seals |
| Verified | Verdigris | `#66897F` | Healthy states, approvals, verified relationships |

Night, Imperial Plum, and Bone should account for roughly 85% of the identity. The remaining colors are functional accents. Avoid bright red, orange, royal blue, and neon.

## Typography

- **Wordmark / Monument:** wide-spaced uppercase monumental grotesk with subtly carved proportions.
- **System:** neutral contemporary sans for navigation, body copy, and interfaces.
- **Signal:** IBM Plex Mono for provenance, identifiers, permissions, audit states, and timestamps.

Avoid traditional Roman display serifs, faux inscriptions, luxury-fashion typography, and futuristic novelty faces.

## Core language

Primary theme: **The Silent Instrument**

Primary message: **Your systems. Your logic. Your command.**

Supporting messages:

- **Sovereign operating system**
- **Customer-owned by design**
- **From record to action**
- **Provenance. Permission. Execution.**

## Voice

Praetorian speaks like controlled infrastructure: brief, exact, and calm. It does not perform intelligence; it demonstrates custody and consequence.

Prefer:

- “Deploy where you operate.”
- “Every action traceable to its source.”
- “Make the operating model executable.”
- “Keep the logic that makes the institution yours.”

Avoid conquest language, Roman slogans, mystical AI language, security fear tactics, and generic promises to unlock data.

## Image direction

- Subterranean and on-premises infrastructure
- Archival records and physical controls
- Human operators at consequential decision points
- Oxidized metals, dry paper, stone, glass, and blacked-out terminals
- Controlled, low-saturation lighting with rare violet or verdigris functional signals

Do not use Roman reenactment, soldiers, statues, columns, desert imagery, generic server racks, glowing blue holograms, or cyberpunk environments.

## Reference takeaway

The relevant lesson from cinematic house heraldry is structural, not stylistic: a faction becomes credible when its emblem is a severe, reproducible silhouette embedded into a believable material culture. Praetorian applies that principle through its own historical scorpion association and sovereign-software story; it does not reproduce any entertainment-franchise symbol, layout, or palette.
