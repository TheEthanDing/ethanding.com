# Archimedes Systems

## First-principles idea

Complex organizations do not need more dashboards. They need a faithful model of the forces, relationships, constraints, and feedback loops that determine what happens next.

Archimedes Systems helps operators understand the whole system, identify the point of leverage, test the consequences of intervention, and execute the right action with guardrails.

## Brand position

**Intelligence with leverage.**

For organizations operating across fragmented, regulated, and difficult data environments, Archimedes Systems is an operational intelligence platform that models how the enterprise actually works and reveals where action will have the greatest effect.

## Core message

**Find the point of leverage.**

Supporting line: **Turn complex systems into decisive action.**

Alternative campaign line: **Understand the system. Then move it.**

## The Fulcrum Spiral

The identity combines an Archimedean spiral, a lever, a fulcrum, and a single action point.

- The spiral represents observation, accumulated context, and complex systems.
- The lever represents the disproportionate effect of a well-chosen intervention.
- The fulcrum represents a grounded operational model.
- The vermilion point represents decisive action.

The mark should remain flat and geometric. It should never be treated as an antique emblem or surrounded by ornamental Greek imagery.

## Color

| Role | Name | Hex | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Primary | Deep Aegean | `#123B49` | Depth, systems thinking, Mediterranean context |
| Ground | Papyrus | `#EFE5CF` | Proof, clarity, an editorial working surface |
| Structural | Aged Bronze | `#A66A3F` | Mechanics, instruments, intellectual heritage |
| Action | Vermilion | `#D94B32` | The point of intervention |
| Neutral | Graphite | `#171C20` | Technical copy and product contrast |
| State | Sea Glass | `#77A99A` | Relationships, verified paths, healthy state |

Papyrus and Deep Aegean should dominate. Bronze describes structure. Sea Glass shows relationships. Vermilion appears only where the user, system, or message takes action.

## Typography

- **Manrope** — navigation, product UI, body copy, and practical communication.
- **Newsreader** — headlines, point-of-view writing, and moments of intellectual emphasis.
- **IBM Plex Mono** — model labels, evidence, system state, policy, and audit metadata.

The serif makes the brand analytical and human without becoming academic. The sans and mono keep the product modern and operational.

## Operating method

1. **Observe** — connect fragmented operational facts.
2. **Model** — represent entities, relationships, constraints, and policy.
3. **Test** — simulate consequences before changing the real system.
4. **Act** — execute the highest-leverage intervention with guardrails.

Governance, auditability, and customer ownership run beneath every stage.

## Graphic language

- Measured arcs express incomplete knowledge becoming coherent.
- Nodes and relationship lines describe the operating model.
- Tangents and counterweights show forces and constraints.
- Dashed paths represent scenarios rather than observed truth.
- A vermilion vector marks the chosen intervention.
- Papyrus space is functional: diagrams should never fill every available area.

Avoid decorative equations, gears, circuit boards, Greek columns, laurel wreaths, portraits, and generic AI imagery.

## Voice

Archimedes sounds curious, exact, and calm. It makes complex systems understandable without pretending they are simple.

Prefer:

- “See the forces shaping the outcome.”
- “Model the organization as it actually operates.”
- “Test consequences before committing action.”
- “Intervene where change compounds.”
- “Keep policy and judgment in the loop.”

Avoid grand claims about omniscience, magic, disruption, or autonomous transformation.

## Homepage hierarchy

1. **Find the point of leverage.**
2. Model the relationships, constraints, and workflows that drive your organization—then act where it matters.
3. Primary action: **Explore the platform**
4. Secondary action: **Discuss a deployment**
5. Proof sequence: observe the whole → model what matters → act with precision
